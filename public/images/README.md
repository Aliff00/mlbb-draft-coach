# Background Images

Place your custom MLBB arena or stage background wallpaper in this folder:

- **Recommended Path**: `public/images/arena-bg.jpg` (or `arena-bg.png`)
- **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen (e.g. `1920x1080` or `2560x1440` or `3840x2160`)

The app in `src/App.tsx` is already wired to automatically detect and display `/images/arena-bg.jpg` or `/images/arena-bg.png` behind the draft arena with an esports ambient blend.
